What the real data taught us

Clouds fool raw entropy

On real VIIRS quick-looks, clouds are the most textured scenes. Fusing entropy with the onboard land mask fixes the ranking.

Median quick-look entropy by real scene type [bit/px]

Share of all scenes in brackets. Cloud is both the most common and the most "information-rich" looking.

Value captured by the top 30 % of scenes (held-out days)

Raw entropy triages worse than random. The E-LOTUS estimator comes within a few points of a label-aware oracle.
